# Weekly Activity Review

**Status:** Implemented in the current follow-up branch; CI verification pending  
**Scope:** A simulation-backed weekly review for optional activities, background responsibilities, stamina allocation, and combined wellbeing outcomes.

## Product intent

The weekly plan is a list of choices, not a calendar. Players toggle activities from the workspace where they belong. There is no Schedule destination, day grid, period reservation, or player-managed school/work timetable.

Education and employment remain ongoing background responsibilities. Attendance, work progression, and their pressure on the character are resolved automatically each week. They contribute to workload without creating calendar blocks.

## Weekly plan and resolution

- Personal activities are selected in their owning workspaces and collected in one weekly plan.
- The Advance review lists the exact selected actions and their direct costs/rewards.
- The plan is resolved in a stable selection order; stamina availability must never depend on the order in which the player toggled actions.
- The review is a read-only forecast generated by the same deterministic resolver used for the real weekly resolution.
- The plan clears only after successful resolution. A stale or unaffordable action must not partially consume the week.

## Stamina budget

Stamina is a weekly allocation budget. Selecting an activity reserves its stamina cost; it does not immediately reduce the character's current stamina.

`Available stamina = current stamina + planned recovery`

`Remaining activity budget = available stamina - planned activity costs`

- A plan is valid only when remaining activity budget is zero or higher.
- Rest and medical recovery contribute to the planned recovery pool.
- Recovery is credited before activity costs, so adding rest after a demanding activity still balances the same weekly plan.
- Recovery activities remain selectable even when all current stamina has been allocated.
- The review must show current stamina, total activity cost, planned recovery, and projected ending stamina.
- The forecast must never show negative stamina. If the plan is over budget, resolving the week is blocked and the player can add recovery or remove activities.
- Natural weekly fatigue/recovery is applied separately from the reserved activity budget and is reflected in the final forecast.
- Recovery effects such as health, happiness, stress, and treatment progress are applied exactly once.

## Workload and wellbeing

Stamina is not the only limit. Background school/work responsibilities and optional activities contribute to weekly workload. Excessive workload increases stress and can reduce health, focus, learning efficiency, work performance, and recovery. Rest can restore stamina and reduce pressure, but it does not erase the workload that caused stress in the first place.

Do not turn this into a universal action quota. Players can choose a demanding week, but the combined consequences should be legible and meaningful.

## Save migration

- Upgrade existing save data non-destructively.
- Preserve character identity, age/date, education records, employment, finance, family, history, and valid selected personal actions.
- Remove legacy calendar commitments and their date/period placements.
- Never regenerate school or work time-slot reservations during migration, enrollment, resume, hiring, job changes, or resignation.
- Keep a recovery copy of the exact pre-migration payload before writing the migrated save.
- Old date/period fields may remain as compatibility-only fields on legacy activity records until a later data-model cleanup; they must not affect gameplay or validation.

## Lifestyle and money

Money changes the quality of available choices, not only the size of a balance. The separate [Lifestyle Economy](LIFESTYLE_ECONOMY.md) specification defines how food quality, everyday goods, safety, comfort, and optional experiences should create meaningful budget-to-affluence progression.

## Acceptance criteria

1. No Schedule navigation item or timetable workspace is present.
2. Education and work have no visible day/period schedule; both continue to progress automatically.
3. All planned activities remain toggleable from their owning workspaces, with a visible PLANNED seal that does not block the toggle.
4. The weekly review shows activity costs, recovery, projected stamina, combined wellbeing changes, and cash impact.
5. Recovery can offset activity costs regardless of selection order.
6. An over-budget plan cannot resolve and never previews negative stamina.
7. Previewing does not mutate the save; resolving applies each cost/reward exactly once.
8. Existing saves migrate with a backup; selected actions and all non-scheduling character data remain intact.
9. Tests cover migration, no timetable generation, budget boundaries, recovery order-independence, exactly-once recovery, and automatic education/work progression.
