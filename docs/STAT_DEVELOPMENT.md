# Age-aware Stat Development

Age controls each stat's broad development ceiling; individual potential narrows it; activity access and player choices determine how quickly the character approaches it. Natural development resolves once per successful week and consumes no stamina. Activity and environmental gains are bounded by the effective ceiling. Existing above-ceiling values on older saves are preserved.

Initial provisional ceiling anchors vary by stat: infant 8–25, age 3 20–35, age 6 37–48, age 13 59–68, age 16 72–78, age 18 80–85, age 25 90–94, age 40 96–98. Physical capabilities peak and decline earlier; cognitive, social, and self-management capabilities remain developable later.

## Required simulation matrix
- Same seeds at ages 0, 3, 6, 13, 16, 18, 25, 45, 65, and 80.
- Supportive, structured, strained, and neglectful family cultures.
- Natural-only development versus education, exercise, and social activity.
- Low-opportunity and high-opportunity environments.
- High-potential and low-potential characters.
- Long runs to check ceilings, plateaus, and age-related decline.

Track starting values, weekly natural gains, activity gains, effective ceilings, time to ceiling, and decline. Confirm childhood growth is automatic, adult growth remains possible, and repeated activities cannot exploit gains beyond the effective ceiling. Curves are provisional tuning parameters, not medical or developmental claims.
